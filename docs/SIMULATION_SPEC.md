# Simulation specification

Runtime agents and vehicles are derived from `CityDocumentV1`. They are not persisted. M3.6/M3.6.1 is a bounded pedestrian layer; M3.6.2 is a bounded vehicle layer. M3.6.3 adds component-based pedestrians and safe crossing admission (ADR-015), still without a city economy or schedules.

- **SIM-001:** Agents are reconstructible from the document seed and PedestrianNetwork. They are never CityEntity records or exported fields and do not change document hashes.
- **SIM-002:** M3.6.3 walkability includes sidewalk corridors, complete junction crossings and reachable park interiors. Other carriageway, lots and courtyards remain excluded. Existing footprints and the valid mask constrain the whole pedestrian disk, except curb-class street furniture (ADR-0016 / GEN-030) and park-surface/garnish (ADR-0017 / GEN-032): catalog `street-furniture` or `street-utility` whose larger footprint axis is less than 0.25 cells is not a pedestrian obstacle; plaza path strips, flowers, grass, small pots and park signs are also not obstacles. Dumpsters, construction, trees, large bushes, statues, planters and large pots remain obstacles. ADR-015 supersedes the earlier park exclusion. Pathfinding is not rewritten; reconstruction uses the non-obstacle allowlist.
- **SIM-003:** A* runs in @city/core on the hybrid network with length cost and deterministic ID ties. Park paths use a 0.1-cell grid, diagonal corner checks and visibility simplification. Time and seed are inputs. Wander destinations must be reachable sidewalk or park points, never crossing interiors.
- **SIM-004:** M3.6.3 replaces cell/lane reservation with continuous disk avoidance, snapshot proposals and swept validation (SIM-022). Pedestrians preserve their destination when yielding and replanning; blocked crossings are penalized after ten seconds without forcing entry.
- **SIM-005:** M3.8 poses every NPC with `@city/procedural-animation` from actual fixed-tick motion (`idle` / `walk` / `run` / `greet`). Kenney Idle/Run clips may remain in generated GLBs but runtime playback does not consume them; jump remains unused. The four seeded skins remain. Walking speed is 0.33 cells/s with seeded ±10% variation and 1–3 second arrival pauses. Catalog body uniformScale is 0.5625 of the previous 0.32-unit body (another 25% reduction from 0.75). Rendering adds the `roads:tile-low` slab height so feet rest on the paved surface; pose, radius and navigation do not change.
- **SIM-006:** Default Auto/high agent counts follow map size (8/12/16/24 for 64/96/128/256). A 96×96 Auto/high view therefore shows 12 agents (Low may halve the default). The city panel may override pedestrians independently in 0–64 without persisting them. This layer must not break AC-010.
- **SIM-007:** Navigation policy, runtime components and avatar remain separate. The grid walk-policy queries remain available for generator compatibility tests, but the old cell-step mover is removed. The default runtime uses PedestrianNetwork and NpcWorld.
- **SIM-008:** After blocks are flood-filled, every **habitable** block cell 4-adjacent to occupied road becomes a 1-cell sidewalk belonging to that manzana (parks with an interior included). Mask-edge sides with no road stay incomplete. Remnants too thin to keep an interior after the ring are pocket parks without sidewalks.
- **SIM-009:** Pedestrian crossings are local T and 4-way Kenney `*-path` tiles and their immediate occupied-road arms, arterial/collector T and 4-way `road-intersection` / `road-crossroad` cells (real crossings only) and their arms, sidewalk-corner road cells, and the four approach cells of a 3×3 roundabout (including local 4-ways that took that mesh). There are no mid-block crosswalks. Dual-carriageway through-segments are not crossings.
- **SIM-010:** Sidewalk routes prefer the right side with a 0.16-cell offset when valid. These are preferences, not capacity-two cell reservations. Spawn uses non-overlapping sidewalk positions; parks permit continuous free movement.

- **SIM-011:** Vehicles are reconstructible from the open document seed and `DriveNetwork` rebuilt from persisted `RoadTopology` plus catalog profiles. They are not `CityEntity` values, do not appear in export, and do not change generator hashes except through persisted topology and any GEN-029 tile repairs.
- **SIM-012:** Directed lanes and allowed maneuvers derive from persisted `RoadTopology`. Right-hand traffic; one-cell streets carry both senses and paired avenues one sense per cell. Dual nudos may connect two same-facing entry/exit ports. Internal stubs stay two-way with a physical return, and the peer port that feeds a terminal is restored to two-way so that return has a successor. Junctions, including dual-avenue nudos, are resolved units whose turns connect entry and exit ports inside the junction. No mid-block U-turns; internal terminals need validated return geometry.
- **SIM-013:** A* searches directed segments with length cost (zero heuristic is admissible) and deterministic ID tie-breaks. Destinations are reachable lane segments or external exits, never intermediate turn connectors. RNG and time are inputs. External exits recycle the same runtime vehicle at another portal entry, keeping the SIM-016 budget.
- **SIM-014:** Motion is separate from pedestrians and rendering. Linear and cubic Bézier curves have matching endpoint positions/tangents and deterministic arc-length tables from adaptive subdivision; yaw follows the derivative. Vehicle speed remains `(1.85 / 3) * 2` cells/s, independent of NPC speed. Full elapsed distance is consumed across segments with no discarded remainder at joins. Vehicles ghost through each other and pedestrians.
- **SIM-015:** A 3×3 roundabout is a shared continuous CCW ring with tangent entry/exit connectors. Its measured raised central island is never traversed.
- **SIM-016:** Default Auto/high vehicle counts follow the same curve as SIM-006. The city panel may override vehicles independently in 0–64 without persisting them. Low quality may reduce the default. This layer must not break AC-010.
- **SIM-017:** Road profiles come from measured catalog surfaces, not occupancy rectangles. Conservative swept geometry must contain every catalog body at its scale and pivot within the carriageway and valid mask. Numerical tolerance is 0.001 cells (`DRIVE_TOLERANCE`). Opposing-lane encroachment during a tight turn is allowed; curb, island, sidewalk, untraversable ground, and mask exit are not.
- **SIM-018:** Every required lane has a continuation, valid terminal return, or external exit. Invalid new cities are rejected after bounded deterministic retries with location and reason; no silent exclusion of roads or maneuvers, and no partially valid city.
- **SIM-019:** Crossing references associate the pedestrian network with traffic geometry. M3.6.3 predicts vehicles to admit safe complete pedestrian crossings (SIM-023); vehicles still ghost and do not brake or yield. Network, mobile state and rendering remain separate.

Kenney City Kit scale stays native (one map cell = one world unit). Agent GLB scale starts native; if the character is disproportionate versus `commercial:building-a` (~1.3 units tall), one override factor applies to agents only. Vehicle GLB scale uses per-entry `uniformScale` so body length fits about 0.54 cells. The `cars` pack does not catalog loose Kenney wheel GLBs; runtime vehicles draw the child wheel nodes already in each body GLB as static meshes.

## M3.6.3 runtime contracts

- **SIM-020:** One reconstructible PedestrianNetwork exposes sidewalk corridors, crossing edges, park grid nodes, obstacles, blocked samples and surface queries. Unreachable parks stay excluded without generator edits.
- **SIM-021:** Position is continuous state. Shared curve math rounds validated paths; bounded acceleration and yaw follow actual movement. Replans start at the current pose. No lane-offset snapping, teleport recovery or off-mask motion.
- **SIM-022:** Radius is 0.12 cells at the new scale. A spatial index finds neighbors; deterministic proposals prefer right-side passing, predict encounters and validate swept disks against other proposals and static space. If unsafe, yield; no mesh physics.
- **SIM-023:** Complete crossing edges reserve their corridor and exit. Prediction uses cloned actual vehicle ticks, including route changes and portals, with catalog pivot-aware conservative body circles. The horizon includes travel, acceleration/settling allowance and one-second safety margin; prediction is conservative. Retry admission every 0.5 seconds. After ten seconds seek another route to the same goal; otherwise keep waiting. Pending orders and cancellation finish an admitted crossing first.
- **SIM-024:** NpcWorld stores separate identity, pose, locomotion, navigation, behavior, crossing and appearance collections. NpcOrder is moveTo(point) or wait(seconds), with pending/active/completed/cancelled/failed outcomes and reasons. Wandering issues these same orders. No framework ECS, building access tasks, agendas or persistence.
- **SIM-025:** A shared 1/60 s clock advances pedestrians and vehicles; render interpolates poses. At most eight ticks run per frame and backlog is retained. Hidden tabs pause both layers without catching up hidden time. Pause/Resume/Step controls the shared clock; Step advances one tick. Surviving population IDs keep their state. Population changes apply on active ticks; adding pedestrians or vehicles is deferred while any NPC owns a crossing, so an admission prediction is not invalidated.
- **SIM-026:** The off-by-default pedestrian overlay and keyboard inspector share the actual network and component snapshots. They expose routes, obstacles, access, blocked areas, radius, neighbors, orders and wait reasons. Diagnostic state is runtime-only and geometry is disposed when disabled.
- **SIM-027:** `NpcOrchestrationConfig` resolves finite bounded partial overrides over defaults: existing seeded walk speed `0.33 cells/s ±10%`, run speed `0.75 cells/s` with the same per-NPC factor, crossing-run enabled, greeting radius `0.9`, deterministic seeded cooldown 8–18 seconds, and manual crossing alignment dot threshold `0.5`. Identical seed, IDs, config, and fixed ticks produce identical control/social/directive state.
- **SIM-028:** `tickNpcWorld` is the sole integrator for autonomous and manual NPCs. Manual camera-relative input creates proposals that pass the existing mask, pedestrian-surface, obstacle and swept-disk separation checks. Crossing input selects only an aligned outgoing edge, uses existing traffic-safe admission, and locks an admitted crossing leg to run speed until complete. Stop/release/route changes defer across that leg.
- **SIM-029:** Stationary autonomous NPC greetings pair deterministically by seed, IDs and fixed ticks, use per-NPC cooldowns, look toward the partner and increment a one-shot wave sequence. Manual commands have priority. Greetings never reserve space, alter routes or interrupt admitted crossings. Directives are `idle`, `walk`, `run`, or `greet` and are driven by actual motion.
- **SIM-030:** Procedural animation may submit a finite, bounded root translation/yaw request. Core validates it atomically against static visibility, pedestrian surface and NPC separation; rejected correction creates no deferred debt. Control, social/directive components and surviving stable IDs persist across population resizing, while missing IDs/document changes clear safely. No runtime tick mutates `CityDocumentV1`.
