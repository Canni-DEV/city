# Simulation specification

Runtime agents and vehicles are derived from `CityDocumentV1`. They are not persisted. M3.6/M3.6.1 is a bounded pedestrian layer; M3.6.2 is a bounded vehicle layer. Neither is a city simulator.

- **SIM-001:** Agents are reconstructible from the open document seed and the pedestrian walk graph. They are not `CityEntity` values, do not appear in export, and do not change generator hashes (sidewalk cells and path tiles do, because they are document fields).
- **SIM-002:** The walkable graph is sidewalk cells union junction crossings (one 4-connected component). Carriageway cells that are not crossings are not walkable. Lots, courtyards, and park interiors are not walkable.
- **SIM-003:** Pathfinding is A* on that graph in `@city/core`. Randomness and time are function inputs. Destinations are other sidewalk cells; arriving picks a new destination. Crossings are traversed, not chosen as goals.
- **SIM-004:** Each agent reserves its current `(cell, lane)` and the `(cell, lane)` it is stepping into. Two lanes per cell allow opposite-direction passing. If both lanes of the next cell are reserved, the agent waits; if they stay reserved, it replans A* to a new destination. There is no mesh-vs-mesh physics.
- **SIM-005:** Clips are Kenney `idle` (stopped) and `run` (moving). `jump` is cataloged and unused. The four protagonist skins (`skaterMaleA`, `skaterFemaleA`, `cyborgFemaleA`, `criminalMaleA`) are assigned with RNG derived from the city seed. Default travel speed is about one-third of a cell per second so the run cycle reads as a walk.
- **SIM-006:** A 96×96 Auto/high view shows 8–16 agents. Low quality may reduce the count. This layer must not break AC-010.
- **SIM-007:** Movement is a walk policy separate from animation. M3.6.1 binds the default policy to the sidewalk/crossing graph; `createRoadWalkPolicy` remains injectable. A later player may use a different policy without replacing the avatar.
- **SIM-008:** After blocks are flood-filled, every **habitable** block cell 4-adjacent to occupied road becomes a 1-cell sidewalk belonging to that manzana (parks with an interior included). Mask-edge sides with no road stay incomplete. Remnants too thin to keep an interior after the ring are pocket parks without sidewalks.
- **SIM-009:** Pedestrian crossings are local T and 4-way Kenney `*-path` tiles and their immediate occupied-road arms, arterial/collector T and 4-way `road-intersection` / `road-crossroad` cells (real crossings only) and their arms, sidewalk-corner road cells, and the four approach cells of a 3×3 roundabout (including local 4-ways that took that mesh). There are no mid-block crosswalks. Dual-carriageway through-segments are not crossings.
- **SIM-010:** Each walkable cell has two logical lanes. Agents prefer the right-hand lane for their heading (world offset about 0.2 units). Spawn uses unique `(cell, lane)` pairs on sidewalk cells only.

- **SIM-011:** Vehicles are reconstructible from the open document seed and `DriveNetwork` rebuilt from persisted `RoadTopology` plus catalog profiles. They are not `CityEntity` values, do not appear in export, and do not change generator hashes except through persisted topology and any GEN-029 tile repairs.
- **SIM-012:** Directed lanes and allowed maneuvers derive from persisted `RoadTopology`. Right-hand traffic; one-cell streets carry both senses and paired avenues one sense per cell. Junctions, including dual-avenue nudos, are resolved units whose turns connect entry and exit ports inside the junction. No mid-block U-turns; internal terminals need validated return geometry.
- **SIM-013:** A* searches directed segments with length cost (zero heuristic is admissible) and deterministic ID tie-breaks. Destinations are reachable lane segments or external exits, never intermediate turn connectors. RNG and time are inputs. External exits recycle the same runtime vehicle at another portal entry, keeping the SIM-016 budget.
- **SIM-014:** Motion is separate from pedestrians and rendering. Linear and cubic Bézier curves have matching endpoint positions/tangents and deterministic arc-length tables from adaptive subdivision; yaw follows the derivative. Speed is twice `DEFAULT_AGENT_SPEED`. Full elapsed distance is consumed across segments with no discarded remainder at joins. Vehicles ghost through each other and pedestrians.
- **SIM-015:** A 3×3 roundabout is a shared continuous CCW ring with tangent entry/exit connectors. Its measured raised central island is never traversed.
- **SIM-016:** A 96×96 Auto/high view shows 8–16 vehicles (same count curve as SIM-006). Low quality may reduce the count. This layer must not break AC-010.
- **SIM-017:** Road profiles come from measured catalog surfaces, not occupancy rectangles. Conservative swept geometry must contain every catalog body at its scale and pivot within the carriageway and valid mask. Numerical tolerance is 0.001 cells (`DRIVE_TOLERANCE`). Opposing-lane encroachment during a tight turn is allowed; curb, island, sidewalk, untraversable ground, and mask exit are not.
- **SIM-018:** Every required lane has a continuation, valid terminal return, or external exit. Invalid new cities are rejected after bounded deterministic retries with location and reason; no silent exclusion of roads or maneuvers, and no partially valid city.
- **SIM-019:** Derived pedestrian crossing IDs and zones associate lane segments with pedestrian paths for future simulation. Existing pedestrian movement/reservation rules are unchanged; no ECS, signals, reservations, or yielding is implemented. Network data, mobile state, and render stay separate.

Kenney City Kit scale stays native (one map cell = one world unit). Agent GLB scale starts native; if the character is disproportionate versus `commercial:building-a` (~1.3 units tall), one override factor applies to agents only. Vehicle GLB scale uses per-entry `uniformScale` so body length fits about 0.67 cells. Kenney Car Kit bodies are cataloged without wheel meshes in M3.6.2.
